## 02 — Outside the Box
**Reflection prompt:** Why did I assume operators and the layout were fixed?
**Suggested reflection:** Prior classroom experience encourages conventional solutions. Identify explicit constraints and separate them from assumptions. Explore alternatives within real rules.

## 05 — Learning goal
Develop practical ethical hacking, Linux, networking, web/API security and vulnerability reporting skills. Apply them to owned software projects and authorized bug bounty programs. Seek competence before credentials.

## 06 — Learning Pyramid
Research reading, demonstration, discussion, hands-on practice and teaching. Compare sources, distinguish claims from evidence and record references. Do not treat fixed retention percentages as proven.

## 07 — Creativity and problem solving
Question assumptions, divide complex tasks, generate alternatives, test ideas, record results and take breaks.

## 12 — Documentation
Suggested penetration test report structure:
- Executive summary, authorized scope and methodology.
- Findings with severity, evidence, impact and reproduction details.
- Remediation recommendations, limitations and conclusion.

## 13 — Organization
Compare Scrum (structured teamwork), Agile (flexibility), to-do lists (simplicity), Kanban (visual progress), and bullet journals (customization). For individual study, try a to-do list and Kanban.

## 15 — Attention observation
Suggested log columns: date, study topic, location, sleep, session length, breaks, concentration and observations. This is a proposed activity, not recorded results.

## 18 — ROQ practice
**Example:** An owned application cannot connect to its database.
- Position: developer.
- Object: database connection.
- Known: application starts.
- Unknown: exact cause of connection failure.
- Other position: database administrator.

**Precise question:** Is the connection failing because of configuration, authentication or service availability?

**Review answer:** A useful question is precise enough to clarify relationships and guide the next investigative step.

Try applying ROQ independently to three to five personal situations.
