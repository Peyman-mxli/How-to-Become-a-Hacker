12 — IPv6 Addresses

Offline hexadecimal IPv6 notation and allocation calculators. No network traffic, no dependencies. Dotted IPv4 tails and zone identifiers are not supported by this educational parser.

IPv6 expansion and canonical compression

Canonical RFC 5952: longest run of at least two zero groups; first when tied; lowercase and no leading zeros.

IPv6 prefix allocator

BigInt arithmetic handles 128-bit values exactly. Addresses are normalized to the parent prefix. No IPv4-style broadcast subtraction.
